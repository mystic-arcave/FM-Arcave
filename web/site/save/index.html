Keep your game safe

Your story, scores, tickets, Rock's wardrobe and your music room live in one small save: on your FM-1, or in this browser for the game on the PLAY page. Copy it here, keep the file somewhere safe, and put it back whenever you need to, on the same FM-1 or another one.

    How it works

    Nothing is lost by trying

    BACK UPThe page asks your FM-1 for its save, the way PLAY WITH MY FM-1 does, and lets it go again. For a moment the FM-1 shows its browser screen; nothing on it changes.
    PUT BACKReplaces the save on your FM-1, or the browser game's. What was there is copied into this list first, marked AUTOMATIC, so a put-back can itself be undone. The FM-1 starts again from the save it was given.
    AUTOMATICA copy is also made each time an FM-1 joins the PLAY page, and before FORGET THE SAVE there. The newest eight automatic copies are kept; the ones you make yourself stay until you delete them.
    OTHER VERSIONSA backup from an older FM-ARCAVE goes onto a newer one: it is brought up to date on the way, as the FM-1 does itself after an update. A backup from a newer version needs the FM-1 updated first, on the INSTALL page.
    ON THE FM-1 ITSELFAn FM-1 with an FM-ARCAVE newer than 0.28 also keeps a spare copy of its save, made each time it is switched on. If a save is ever cut short by the power going, it starts from the spare and says so.
    THE FILEA small text file (JSON) that holds the save and a checksum. It is yours: copy it, send it to another computer, open it here again.
