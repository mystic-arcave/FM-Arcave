Mystic machines and instruments

Three ledges, four instruments, yours to fill

The Arcave has three ledges with a machine on each, and a music room with four instruments. A new install brings CRYSTAL ROAD, JUICE WORKS and NIGHT BLOOM, and ROOTS, THE GEODE, MUSHROOM RACK and CRYSTAL SEAM. Here you choose what stands where, and load machines and instruments the firmware does not carry into your FM-1, the way presets go into a synth. Nothing in the game changes until you change it here.

How it works

Slots, like presets

THREE SLOTSEach ledge has a slot in the FM-1's memory, 40 KB of it, for one loaded machine. The three machines the firmware carries need no slot: they are always there, and a ledge can show any of them.
THE MUSIC ROOMIts four places have a slot each as well, for one loaded instrument. The firmware's instrument of a place is always there, and the loaded one stands in for it when you choose so. Each keeps its own layout, knobs and lesson, so nothing is lost when you swap; that place's part of the loop is wiped, because it is made of the other one's sounds. Friends who play together in the room need the same instruments in the same places.
LOADINGThe page sends the machine over the same link PLAY WITH MY FM-1 uses. The FM-1 shows its browser screen meanwhile, checks every byte of what arrived, and starts again with the machine on its ledge. Its best score and tickets are kept with your save.
NEVER STUCKA machine that arrives damaged, or is made for a newer FM-ARCAVE, is not run: the ledge keeps the firmware's own machine. If the FM-1 ever restarts inside a loaded machine, loaded machines rest until it has been switched off and on.
THE BROWSER'S GAMEThe game on the PLAY page has ledges of its own, kept in this browser. Close PLAY before changing them here.
YOUR SAVEWhich machine stands where is part of your save, so a backup remembers it. The loaded machines themselves are not in the save: load them again from here.
MAKE ONEA machine is one small C file, and so is an instrument. docs/MACHINES.md tells how; GLOW CAPS and THE SPRING are the examples to start from.

FM-ARCAVE is free software, GPL-3.0-only. Loading writes only to the seven slots kept for machines and instruments; one from anywhere but this page is code you run on your FM-1 at your own risk.
