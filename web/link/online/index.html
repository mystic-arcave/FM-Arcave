Down the well, together from far away

UNDERSONG over the internet. Make a private lobby, send its code to a friend, and both of you roll Rock down the well: on a real FM-1 by USB or Bluetooth, or on the emulator in this browser. Up to three computers in a lobby.

1 ON THIS COMPUTER

Plug your FM-1 in and press USE MY FM-1, or switch its Bluetooth on (GLO) and press ADD AN FM-1 BY BLUETOOTH, or open the emulator. Then roll Rock to the well and go down: they show here once they are in it.

2 THE LOBBY

OR JOIN A FRIEND'S

LOBBY CODE: ONLY THOSE YOU GIVE IT TO CAN JOIN

WHAT PASSES

Only UNDERSONG's own messages: who is in the well, the keys and dials of each player, and the clock of the one who leads. The lobby server passes them on and knows nothing of the game. No sound or picture is sent: each FM-1 plays the game itself.

DISTANCE

Each FM-1 judges its own player against its own clock, so the time messages take never costs you a note. It shows only as your friends' notes sounding a little late. The ping by each name is the round trip from that computer to the lobby server.

REAL OR EMULATED

Any mix works: an FM-1 against an emulator, two FM-1s at your end and one at your friend's. Every FM-1 on this computer, by USB or by Bluetooth, and every emulator window in this browser goes into the lobby. An FM-1 that is in both ways is counted once. Keep the emulator's own MIDI switched off here, or it is heard twice.

PRIVATE

Lobbies are not listed anywhere: a lobby is found by its code alone. It closes when it has been empty for half an hour. If the connection breaks, this page joins again by itself.

UNDERSONG's messages are control changes 102 to 118 on MIDI channel 16; docs/LINK.md has them and the lobby's own protocol. The lobby server is server/ in the source.
