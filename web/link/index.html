HOW TO CONNECT

By USB: plug each FM-1 into this computer (a USB hub is fine). By Bluetooth: switch Bluetooth on in the FM-1's settings (GLO), press ADD AN FM-1 BY BLUETOOTH here and pick it from the browser's list, once for each FM-1; each shows as FM-ARCAVE and four characters of its own. Any mix of the two works, and an FM-1 that is in both ways is counted once. A browser tab running the emulator with MIDI on counts as an FM-1 too, through a virtual MIDI port.

WHAT STAYS IN TIME

Tempo, beat and bar. A follower slides into place over a beat or two, never jumps mid-note. Change the tune on the leader (ALGORITHM) and every FM-1 takes its tempo. With LINK on, all of FM-ARCAVE's tunes play in one shared key, so different tunes on different machines still agree.

HOW CLOSE

The page measures each FM-1's delay with a ping and sends each one the clock moved ahead by that much. "OFF BY" shows how far each FM-1 is from the leader. Bluetooth takes longer than a cable and not the same time twice, so the page pings a Bluetooth FM-1 more often and goes by the quickest answers. These numbers come from the emulator so far, not from four FM-1s on a table.

UNDERSONG, FOR TWO OR THREE

The game for two or three FM-1s, down the well in the Arcave's floor. Bring them all in here, by USB or by Bluetooth, start the hub, and roll each Rock to the well: this page passes what each player does to the others, and the clock of the one that leads. With three in the well, every game is played for three. LINK does not have to be on for it. Friends on other computers: the ONLINE tab.

WITHOUT THIS PAGE

An FM-1 with LINK on also follows a plain MIDI clock (USB, Bluetooth or the TRS jack) from a DAW or a drum machine, and START sets it to bar one. A computer's own Bluetooth MIDI settings can connect an FM-1 too (on a Mac: Audio MIDI Setup); it then comes in here as a MIDI port, like a USB one.

The messages are control changes 102 to 118 on MIDI channel 16; docs/LINK.md has the whole protocol. Nothing leaves this computer.
